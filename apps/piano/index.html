Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Thank you for using Piano. This Privacy Policy explains how the application ("Piano", "the App") handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

You are not required to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal data.

Device Data

The App does not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports, or diagnostic information.

Audio

The App generates piano sounds locally on your device. It does not record audio or access your microphone.

Local Storage

If the App stores settings or preferences, they are saved only on your device and are never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Internet Connection

The App does not require an Internet connection to function.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services for collecting user data. In particular, it does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • User authentication services
  • Cloud storage
  • Tracking technologies

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users of all ages. Since no personal information is collected, no information from children under the age of 13 is knowingly collected.

Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal data is stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with an updated "Last updated" date.
